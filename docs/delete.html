Account deletion (English)

To delete your Orun Note (옳은Note) account, open the app and go to Settings (설정) → Delete account (계정 삭제). Deletion is immediate and also removes textbooks and notes stored on that device.

If you can't use the app, email 5554ksj2@gmail.com with the subject "Orun Note account deletion" and the email address you signed in with. We verify the request and delete the account within 7 days.

Deleted: sign-in account (email, account ID), the link to your student code, and email-change requests. Kept for up to 1 year, then deleted: usage logs and crash diagnostics. Student records and submitted homework in the academy's homework system (orunhomework.com) are managed by the academy.
